# Retail-Sales-Profitability-Dashboard
The Retail Sales and Profitability Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, product categories, brands, regions, states, and monthly trends.  The dashboard transforms retail sales data into interactive visual insights.


# Business Objective
The primary objective of this project is to analyze retail sales and profitability data and identify patterns that can support better business decision-making.
The analysis focuses on:
- Analyzing overall sales and profit performance
- Comparing sales across different states and regions
- Identifying profitable product sub-categories
- Analyzing brand-level performance
- Understanding monthly sales and profit trends
- Evaluating the relationship between total sales and total cost
- Analyzing the impact of order status and delivery time


# Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization


# Key Insights
- The dashboard records approximately **1.05 billion in total sales**.
- Total profit is approximately **301.19 million**.
- Sales performance varies across different states.
- Product sub-categories such as **Table, Shoes, Sofa, Jeans, Chair, Monitor, Mobile, Laptop, and T-Shirt** contribute to overall profitability.
- Monthly analysis shows noticeable variations in sales and profit throughout the year.
- Brand-level analysis provides a comparison of profit margin distribution across major brands.
- The relationship between total cost and total sales can be evaluated to understand cost efficiency.
